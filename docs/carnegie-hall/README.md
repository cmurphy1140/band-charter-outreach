## The useful result

The proposed demonstration has five connected deliverables:

1. **An event brief** translating the supplied logistics into a clear explanation of the March 3 experience.
2. **A provider comparison** showing where Troen’s offer is compelling and where differences remain unproven.
3. **An opportunity workbook** with a small, researched set of school and partner examples, evidence, and next steps.
4. **Director and partner materials** showing how the same event supports different business conversations.
5. **An example action brief** connecting proposals, school approvals, contracts, payments, suppliers, and final coordination.

A compact local interactive demonstration can bring these pieces together. Real research stays distinguishable from illustrative workflow activity; a complete operating system is not required to demonstrate substantial value.

## Decisions to preserve

- **March 3, 2027 only.** March 31 and other trips inform the business model; their inventory, prices, and deadlines do not transfer to March 3.
- **Employee-reported participation:** one group. **Ten additional ensembles is a provisional target requiring capacity confirmation**, not ten verified available slots.
- Deliver depth and usefulness without an upfront questionnaire, account audit, relationship export, or capacity exercise for Troen.
- Research schools, tour partners, and existing-relationship clues as available. There is **no established winning channel and no required warm-first sequence**. Relationship checks matter before actual approaches.
- Preserve the research pipeline, source records, spreadsheet formats, and useful technical work. Repair or bypass defects in the paths actually used.
- Compare Troen with performance-event providers separately from evaluating Zoho and our proposed software support.
- Use **logistics and software developer** as the role, preserve the CS-background explanation, and exclude employment positioning.
- Separate the POC from future operating software. The 20–40% preparation-time estimate is an **untested planning hypothesis**.

## Reading the evidence

**Documented** means visible in a dated source, within its scope. **Employee-reported** means information relayed in this project from an employee, without independent reconciliation to booking/payment records. **Recommendation** means a proposed approach. **Illustrative** means an example created to demonstrate behavior. **Unknown** means evidence is absent. Public research is not proof of buying interest.

Use compact labels in demonstration materials and retain detailed qualifications internally. Internal costs, signatures, and private logistics stay out of externally shared examples. The original Word assessment may use different attribution wording; it remains unchanged, and this pack governs current project language.
