# Band charter outreach: repository audit

Audit date: 2026-09-10. Baseline: `be3f9ffd13585bda497edd1707ad9a4e3ffe1a4e` on `main`, matching GitHub when checked. Scope: the existing parade pipeline; Carnegie Hall is a proposed next increment, not an implemented feature. No production code, prospect files, client packet, scheduled jobs, dependencies, or credentials were changed during this audit.

## Decision

Keep this codebase and repair its data-preservation and evidence rules before expanding it or enabling unattended refreshes. It is a useful research pipeline, but the current output is not a verified, contact-ready sales list. Passing parser tests and having a URL in every row do not establish that each populated field belongs to the right school.

Recommended sequence: preserve school identities → prevent destructive refreshes → verify contacts and school matches → correct event dates and labels → regenerate and reconcile outputs → close remaining source gaps → pilot Carnegie Hall.

## Prioritized findings

P1 = repair before another production rebuild, outbound use of affected data, or unattended operation. P2 = repair before expanding or presenting the system as reliably maintained. Confidence distinguishes observed defects from risks demonstrated with synthetic inputs.

### A01 — P1: a rebuild discards enriched identities and their fields

**Location:** `scripts/run_all.py:227–235`; state is filled in `scripts/search_fallback.py:186–204,273–318`.

`carry_over()` looks up the previous record by normalized name AND state. Discovery still has an empty state after research fills the final row's state; on the next merge those keys no longer match. The lookup skips the entire enriched row, including location, school URL, district, enrollment, and notes.

**Observed history:** `01f9278` contains nine researched state placements. `215ce80` removes the state/enrichment for eight retained schools: Cherry Creek, South Paulding, Concord Community, East Coweta, Habersham Central, Mira Costa, Southeast Raleigh, and Decatur Central. Van Nuys is the ninth; its cheer entry was intentionally excluded, so it is not counted as lost retained-school enrichment. Replaying the old rows against current interim data reproduces the eight losses. A same-snapshot rebuild alone misses this regression.

**Fix:** retain an explicit, source-backed resolution from discovery identity to verified school identity before grouping. Preserve ambiguous Westlakes separately. Do not use a global school-name-only fallback. Recover the eight reviewed records from history through that resolution layer and rebuild, rather than hand-editing the final CSV.

**Confidence:** high; current data, history, and executable reproduction agree.

### A02 — P1: one of two director emails belongs to a different role

**Location:** `scripts/enrich.py:287–343`, especially text windows at 310–328; `enrich_row()` at 388–402.

The Woodlands row stores `rhorton@conroeisd.net`. The [cited Conroe page](https://www.conroeisd.net/o/cisd/page/fine-arts) identifies Robert Horton as **Director of Fine Arts** for the district, not the Woodlands band director. A band-director label elsewhere on a district page is insufficient school/person evidence. Existing QA accepts it because the domain matches.

A plain-text two-person example also reproduces the bug: a theatre director's email next to a band director's name is selected and paired with the band director. A structured separate-paragraph mailto example passed; the problem is specifically the broad plain-text/context fallback, not every contact page.

The [Cedar Park source](https://cphs.leanderisd.org/campus_information/fine-arts) does explicitly associate the stored email with its head band director. The unusual stored spelling “Christioher” is printed by the source; do not silently correct it based on the email spelling.

**Fix:** verify role, name, email, and school within one staff record; quarantine unclear district-level records. Store per-contact source and scope. Re-run extraction after the rule is repaired; “existing non-empty fields are never overwritten” otherwise preserves the bad contact indefinitely.

**Confidence:** high; current primary source and synthetic reproduction.

### A03 — P1: source failure can erase previously collected appearances

**Location:** `scrapers/boa.py:107–141`; `scrapers/heb.py:66–86`; `scripts/run_all.py:49–86`.

BOA catches archive/recap failures and returns an empty or partial list. H-E-B catches every blocked page and can also return an empty list. The runner treats any returned list as success, clears the blocked record, and replaces that source's scoped years. A mock BOA outage caused one stored 2026 appearance to be replaced by zero. A full scrape can lose historical years too. This is not a claim that the audit ran a destructive live scrape; it was reproduced only in a temporary directory.

**Fix:** distinguish complete, incomplete, blocked, and valid-empty source/year results. Commit a replacement only after completeness validation. Preserve earlier evidence for failed partitions and report the run as incomplete.

**Confidence:** high, reproduced.

### A04 — P1: normal refreshes can keep reading stale source pages indefinitely

**Location:** `scrapers/common.py:142–148`; `scrapers/wikipedia_rose.py:107`; `scrapers/boa.py:107–135`; `scrapers/hollywood.py:61–73`.

`fetch()` returns cached HTML without an expiry. The year-scoped runner does not pass a freshness requirement to these sources. A cached lineup returns without any network request. News searches do force a refresh, so the defect is not universal, but Wikipedia, BOA archives and other stable URLs can miss new announcements indefinitely. Hard-coded year ranges/categories also constrain future full scrapes (e.g. BOA default ends in 2025).

**Fix:** define a refresh policy for discovery indexes and the requested event years, keep immutable historical cache where appropriate, and report cache timestamps. Derive supported discovery years from the requested run rather than a permanently frozen list.

**Confidence:** high, code plus cache reproduction.

### A05 — P1: SerpAPI failures can persist the key in logs and tracked blocked metadata

**Location:** `scrapers/serpapi.py:53–59`; `scripts/run_all.py:81–83`; `scrapers/common.py:183–190`.

The API key is a query parameter. A requests exception can contain the full URL; the client interpolates that exception into `BlockedSource`, which the runner prints and can write into tracked `_blocked.json`. A synthetic key-bearing connection error survives this path. Stripping fields from successful JSON responses does not protect error paths.

**Fix:** emit sanitized status/error categories and omit credential-bearing request URLs and exception causes from public logs and persisted metadata. Test timeouts, connection failures, non-JSON and API error bodies with a dummy key. Check existing history using a private scan if the real key becomes available.

**Confidence:** high for the exposure path; **no evidence of an actual key leak was found**.

### A06 — P1: early dedupe silently collapses different schools

**Location:** `scripts/run_all.py:64–70`; related heuristic at 145–156.

Interim dedupe uses `(school.lower(), event, year)` and drops state and source evidence. Two Lincoln High Schools in different states at the same event/year become one row before the state-aware merge runs. A temporary-file reproduction retains TX and discards CA. The later stateless-to-one-known-name folding also treats uniqueness inside this incomplete dataset as proof of identity; that is a separate identity risk.

**Fix:** dedupe exact source observations without collapsing state/city distinctions, union evidence, and require explicit resolutions for stateless identity joins. Tests must include same name in two states, two cities within one state, and a stateless third school.

**Confidence:** high for interim collision; stateless assignment risk is inferred from the algorithm.

### A07 — P1: enrichment can cross school boundaries, including a current Pulaski mismatch

**Location:** `scripts/enrich.py:190–239,360–386`; `scripts/search_fallback.py:254–270`; `scripts/qa.py:check_nces_prefix`.

The current Pulaski High School row has city Pulaski, Wisconsin and Red Raider parade evidence, but district Milwaukee School District and its website. The official NCES directory has distinct Pulaski High records: Milwaukee (`550960001235`) and Pulaski Community School District (`551233001600`). Rechecking the current row selects the latter. The old populated district is never overwritten. Existing prefix QA reports zero because it does not fully validate the stored record against school/city identity.

A separate reproduction shows that an unrecognized non-empty city disables the no-city tie downgrade: two identical-name schools receive a 1.0 “unique” choice simply because a city string was supplied, even though it matched neither city.

`_domain()` also reduces both `a.k12.tx.us` and `b.k12.tx.us` to `tx.us`, allowing unrelated districts to look identical. Shared hosting domains have the same problem. Redirected pages are parsed without preserving an explicit school-ownership decision.

**Fix:** store and validate NCES ID and source vintage; distinguish “city provided” from “city matched”; do not auto-fill tied identities; revalidate existing mismatched records. Use an explicit verified host/subdomain allowlist initially, or discuss a public-suffix library before adding a dependency. A matching email suffix alone does not prove a matching school.

**Confidence:** high for Pulaski's contradictory data and both synthetic failures. Other fuzzy exceptions remain review items, not automatic corrections.

### A08 — P1: publication dates are being invented into event years

**Location:** `scrapers/news_east.py:parade_year,headline_row`.

January–March articles without an explicit headline year are assigned the previous year. The stored Biloxi 2017 news appearance is sourced to a [January 30, 2018 WLOX article](https://www.wlox.com/story/37378335/biloxi-high-band-to-march-in-americas-oldest-thanksgiving-day-parade/) announcing a trip that coming Thanksgiving. The article supports a 2018 plan, not a 2017 appearance. Alongside the other 2018 source this inflates Biloxi's count from one supported event-year to two.

**Fix:** require an explicit event-year statement or reviewed article evidence. Unknown event year remains unknown; announcement date is not event date. Keep planned and completed participation separate. Correct this source observation through the parser/evidence layer.

**Confidence:** high; direct source contradiction.

### A09 — P2: totals and sales claims overstate what the evidence establishes

**Location:** `scripts/run_all.py:166–199`; `scripts/score.py:24–77`; `scripts/export.py:95–114`; `docs/packet/Parade-Prospects-Overview.txt:8,32,54,108,122`.

BOA competitions and future invitations enter `parades_marched` and `last_appearance` as if completed parades. There are 20 BOA-only rows; Timber Creek and Clovis have only Rose 2027 recorded. The packet nonetheless describes every school as having executed a parade trip and its top fifty as ready to contact. The “travel signal” comment equates a California parade with a cross-country flight, which does not follow for local schools.

The summary counts **100 Rose event-year appearances across 81 schools**, then labels the 100 as “Schools.” Recency defaults to 2024 permanently; that choice is configurable but does not age automatically with scheduled runs.

**Fix:** distinguish evidence type and participation status; label unique-school counts and event counts accurately; make score components inspectable and use an explicit as-of date. Preserve the current weight settings until Connor chooses the product meaning of recency and qualifying travel evidence.

**Confidence:** high for mislabeled counts/current fields; weight choices are product decisions.

### A10 — P2: refresh reporting and generated artifacts can disagree

**Location:** `scripts/run_all.py:282–305,main`; `scripts/search_fallback.py:353–364`; `workflows/refresh.yml:35–53`; `Makefile:refresh`.

Deleted schools are omitted from refresh diffs; a one-row-to-zero reproduction reports zero new and zero updated. Partial/blocked runs can exit successfully. `make refresh` preserves old score/tier fields but does not rescore or regenerate derived CSV/XLSX files. The workflow likewise does not score/export and is in `workflows/`, so GitHub does not run it. No SerpAPI key is supplied in that workflow. The externally described Claude routine was not independently verified.

**Fix:** stage outputs together, validate before replacement, report added/changed/removed/retained-on-failure records, and propagate incomplete status. Separate data-refresh tests from scheduling. Enable `.github/workflows/` only after these repairs, with an explicit credential/budget configuration and documented delivery behavior.

**Confidence:** high for repository behavior; external routine status unknown.

### A11 — P2: crawl policy has unguarded error and redirect paths

**Location:** `scrapers/common.py:100–116,150–155`; `scripts/enrich.py:88–101`; `scripts/qa.py:check_dead_urls`.

Robots retrieval errors and every non-200 response are treated as permission to crawl, including 503 (reproduced). Automatic redirects are followed without validating each destination against robots/throttle/school scope. NCES downloads and dead-URL checks use direct requests outside the shared policy. The latter is a reachability probe, but that exception is not explicitly documented.

**Fix:** distinguish absent robots from unavailable robots; defer crawling on unavailable policy; check redirect destinations; make archive-download and reachability-probe policies explicit. Download archives to a temporary path and rename only after successful completion so a partial ZIP cannot poison future runs.

**Confidence:** high for code paths; no abusive live crawl was performed.

### A12 — P2: exported source text can become a spreadsheet formula

**Location:** `scripts/export.py:51–69` and CSV output boundaries.

`_write_sheet()` writes strings verbatim. A synthetic school value `=1+1` becomes an openpyxl cell of formula type `f`. Current files contain no such values. This is a future-input risk, not an observed malicious workbook.

**Fix:** explicitly serialize source text as text in XLSX; define a spreadsheet-safe CSV output policy without changing internal canonical values. Test leading `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab/CR, numeric fields, and normal school names.

**Confidence:** high, reproduced.

### A13 — P2: evidence is attached to rows, not individual claims

**Location:** `scripts/enrich.py:287–343,360–405`; `scrapers/heb.py:61`; `scripts/search_fallback.py:107–151`; `scrapers/exclusions.py`.

NCES IDs and source-file vintage are not retained in final records. Booster strings lack their own provenance. School homepages, district arts pages, and unrelated news pages can all become `band_url`. Franklin's row points to an orchestra-camp article and contains “RHS Band Booster”; Westbury points to a district-wide music page. These need school-specific verification, not merely a reachable URL.

H-E-B assigns Houston/TX to every participant without a location statement from the lineup. Organic search accepts “Alhambra Unified School” as the district homepage even though the prospect schema expects a school; its raw band is district-level. Regex-only exclusion checks report zero on the retained list, but that is not an independent eligibility review.

**Fix:** add evidence records keyed by school, field, source URL, extracted value and verification date; retain source identity before normalization; keep district-wide groups and unresolved band nicknames in a review queue. Do not automatically restore Van Nuys's cheer entry or merge the Westlake rows.

**Confidence:** high for provenance/model gaps; Franklin's booster ownership needs further source evidence.

### A14 — P2: the documented suppression and delivery promises exceed implementation

**Location:** `docs/packet/Parade-Prospects-Overview.txt:8,54,108`; `CLAUDE.md:Phase status`; `docs/CODEX_HANDOFF.md:State of the repo`.

The client overview still says 214 programs, describes the Woodlands email as a band-director contact, and says a suppression list is applied. Current output is 206, that contact's role is wrong, and no suppression implementation is present. The handoff calls PR #7 an unmerged draft, but it is merged. Historical quotas and scheduled cloud routines are not current local configuration. The current schema has 21 columns, not the handoff's 20.

**Fix:** make the audit/current status the working reference; implement and test suppression before promising it; reconcile counts and contact claims after repairs. Regenerate the client packet only when Connor chooses to update those deliverables. Do not silently edit the historical narrative into a claim of completed work.

**Confidence:** high for repository discrepancies.

## NCES and source follow-through

The official directory was fetched from [NCES 2023–24 directory ZIP](https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/Data/zip/ccd_sch_029_2324_w_1a_073124.zip), 12,624,979 bytes, with 45,420 rows after the project's open-school/level filter. No new library was installed. The large membership archive was not downloaded; **enrollment counts were checked for formatting/completeness but were not independently verified**.

Of 133 populated districts, six rows need follow-up under today's matching logic: Blue Springs, Pulaski, Clovis, Downingtown, Louisburg, Temple City. Only Pulaski showed a conflicting selected district. Low scores/no match in the others do not prove bad data; school renames, prefix ambiguity and city constraints need source review. Every populated fuzzy match needs an evidence record rather than a threshold alone. Full results are in `nces_checks.json`.

The nine historical state decisions agree with the directory at the school-name/state level using the original exact or panel rule. Cherry Creek's exact school-name match is unique, while the broader substring candidate helper additionally returns Cherry Creek Elevation; those are different matching rules. NCES is a public-school directory, so national uniqueness there does not prove nationwide uniqueness across private schools or establish that an ambiguous parade nickname belongs to that school. Van Nuys's identity is real, but its recorded parade unit was cheer and remains excluded.

The three organic picks were checked against their public sites: Martin Luther King Jr. and Ayala resolve to school sites; Alhambra resolves to a district and needs entity review. Some old URLs now redirect or fail in the web reader. These are retrieval observations, not definitive dead-link classifications.

## Limits and remaining verification

- This was not a live scrape of all parade and school sites. No 206-school end-to-end crawl, 193-URL repeat scan, or paid search refresh was performed.
- The 15-row sample verifies selected source claims, district identities and derived fields; it does not certify every field or every historical appearance. Each unresolved item is listed in the appendix.
- The 188 exclusions were counted and inspected by rule/category, with suspect-name checks. They were not each independently researched against a primary source.
- Membership totals, all six booster organizations, old page snapshots, every organic-search decision, and every raw cached news claim still need field-level verification.
- Real fixtures for blocked sources, restore/failure tests for writing outputs, and platform-specific fresh-environment installation remain outstanding. Existing `.venv` tests do not prove a clean-machine install.
- The client packet was not visually reviewed or regenerated; its text source was checked for claims and drift.
- No external email, Google Sheet, scheduled routine or GitHub workflow was created or modified. No commit or push was made.
