What a line state means

Every priced line carries a state, and every state carries the one word a client-facing document is allowed to print next to it. Silence never implies “booked”.

  • Sourcing · 1Candidates are being shopped. Client word: venue being finalised.
  • Requested · 9A request has gone out; no terms have come back. Client word: requested.
  • Quoted · 9A price and its terms are in. Nothing is held. Client word: quoted.
  • Deposit paid · 1A deposit is paid, a balance remains due. Client word: reserved.
  • Confirmed · 0Supplier confirmed for this date and headcount, nothing outstanding.
  • No supplier line · 7Free time, own-cost meals and stops. No counterparty, so no state.

The moment the record earns its keep

19:30, day one: the proposal named a dinner venue that nobody had secured

The itinerary page prints dinner at the 1909 terminal station complex. The inclusions page sells it by name: “Dinner at the Chattanooga Choo Choo Complex”. At the same time, in pen in the margin, five other venues were being called for that same meal.

The record holds this line as Sourcing, so every generated document prints venue being finalised next to it instead of a name. The candidates below are the shopping list, not a menu shown to the client.

Move two

What the printed proposal was carrying in ballpoint

Rates, comp ratios, deposits, deadlines, who was called and when. All of it lived in the margins of a page that gets reprinted from scratch on every change. Below is what was actually written there, and what the quote system held instead.

  • Group rates need a minimum of 15 admissions, subject to availability.
  • One comp per ten paid, plus a bus driver comp. At 80 paid that is eight earned places plus the driver.

The two kinds of comp

Both written in pen as bare numbers

  • Vendor-earned — free places a supplier grants on its own ratio, such as Rock City’s one per ten plus a driver. These reduce cost.
  • Trip-granted — the three free places the operator gives the group. These are carried by the paying travellers.
  • They move the price in opposite directions. On the page they were two numbers with no labels.

Move three

The director asks for a change

This is the ordinary event that used to cost an afternoon: the page is reprinted from scratch, the pen notes do not survive, and nothing tells you which suppliers the change actually reaches. Pick one of the two asks below, then read move four.

Which change would you like to follow?

Both asks are illustrative. Neither was made by this client. They are written out here to show what the layer does with a change; the trip record itself is unchanged, and no request, hold, payment or cancellation follows from anything on this page.

Why the change is legitimate, by the operator’s own terms

“Pricing is subject to availability and may increase until time of booking. The price is subject to change based on your final number of paying participants.” Exclusions page, both printed iterations

The problem was never that re-quotes happen. The operator’s own terms say they will. The problem is that each one costs an afternoon, and that the afternoon’s work lands on a page that gets thrown away.

The point of the small change

A change that trips no supplier term at all still forces a complete reprint today, and on a reprinted page 08:30 survives, because nothing compares the printed line to the phone note. Five sets of margin notes against seven printed pages, with no mechanism between them, is not a rare situation. It is a Tuesday.

Move five

Every document, regenerated from the record

The record changes once. The documents are outputs, not sources, so they are rebuilt from it rather than edited one by one. Renderers are discovered from a directory: adding one touches no shared file, which is what lets several be built at the same time.

node pipeline/trip/cli.cjs validate --all
node pipeline/trip/cli.cjs render   --all
node pipeline/trip/cli.cjs check    --all   # fails if a tracked output is stale

check is the part that matters here. It compares bytes. A document that no longer matches the record is a failure, not a discrepancy somebody might notice later on a second printing.

Outputs for this trip

Rendered files land in the trip’s own output directory. Renderers are being added in parallel, so what is present depends on what has been rendered at the moment you look.

  • Output directory for this trip Relative path: pipeline/trip/out/west-henderson-chattanooga-2027-04-08/. This link resolves when the page is served from the repository root. Served from demo/trip/ alone, it is outside the server root and will not open — nothing else on the page depends on it.
  • The trip layer contract How a renderer is added, what the record holds, and the privacy rule that applies to every file in the layer.
  • The trip record itself Every figure on this page is in that file, next to the source it came from.

What a generated document may and may not say. Each line prints its state’s client word. The day one dinner prints venue being finalised, not a venue name. Nineteen of the twenty supplier lines on this trip are at sourcing, requested or quoted; one is reserved against a paid deposit; none is confirmed. A document generated from this record cannot quietly promise otherwise, because the word comes from the state and the state comes from the record.

Subtracting across the two tables above: the single supplement over quad at 80 travellers narrows from $487 to $470, and the discount for filling 100 places instead of 80 flattens from $70 to $58.
