# Example follow-up

Fictional group and activity. This is not Wando’s correspondence, approval status, payment history, or booking.

Wednesday, March 3, 2027

## A proposal is requested

Prepare an educational summary and a scoped trip proposal, with assumptions easy to see.

- **Proposal:** Draft the event experience, group needs, and travel scope; leave unapproved prices unquoted.
- **School approval:** Provide a short educational rationale and identify the approval step; approval is not assumed.
- **Contract and payment:** State the agreement and payment milestones in the eventual offer; no agreement or payment is recorded.
- **Travel suppliers:** Check transport and accommodation feasibility before promising arrangements.
- **Final coordination:** Carry confirmed numbers, rooming, and the performance schedule forward when the trip progresses.

## School approval takes longer

Prepare a concise approval summary and review any proposed supplier holds before they become commitments.

- **Proposal:** Keep the latest proposal version and clearly mark which terms still need confirmation.
- **School approval:** Focus on the decision-maker’s actual information need; do not label the group approved.
- **Contract and payment:** Review timing against the proposed terms. Do not record a signed agreement or paid deposit.
- **Travel suppliers:** Recheck any proposed hold or release dates with the supplier; none are invented here.
- **Final coordination:** Defer detailed rooming and final participant work until the trip has a clearer basis.
