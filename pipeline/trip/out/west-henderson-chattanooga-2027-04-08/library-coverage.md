### Constraints these components carry in

**Rock City Gardens**

- Season: Open rain or shine, closed December 24. A higher group minimum applies during the seasonal lights event, so a December trip does not inherit these terms.
- Deadlines: One group leader pays for the whole group five days before arrival. A guided tour needs fourteen days' notice. Cancellations inside five business days are not refundable.
- Access: A trail with narrow rock passages and steps; the operator publishes a separate accessibility page. Bus parking is limited and is arranged with group sales at booking. Parking is free.
- Payment: Cashless since September 2025 and smokeless from July 2026, with ATMs on site. Tell the group before departure.

**Lookout Mountain Incline Railway**

- Deadlines: A flat group deposit secures the booking and the balance falls due ten days out. The deposit figure belongs in the trip record.
- Access: Published as accessible. The last car leaves forty minutes before closing, which sets the latest a group can start the ride.

**Southern Belle Riverboat**

- Deadlines: Not published. Capture the deposit and balance shape the first time this line is booked.
- Access: Not published. Ask before selling the line to a group with wheelchair users.

**Ruby Falls**

- Hours: The first ride of the day is 09:00. With the thirty-minute arrival rule that puts a group on site at 08:30 — so an 08:30 line is the arrival, not the tour, and nothing earlier than a 09:00 tour can be sold.
- Season: Group rates are not available at weekends, on holidays or in July, and June availability is limited. A spring weekday itinerary qualifies; the same itinerary in July does not.
- Deadlines: The group leader prepays for the whole group at least five days ahead, a week's notice is asked for the booking itself, and the group arrives thirty minutes before its tour.
- Access: A glass-front elevator descends 260 feet into the cave, then just under a mile on a paved cavern trail with about fifty steps and handrails. Not wheelchair accessible. Guests should be able to stand for up to an hour and a half.
- Payment: The group leader prepays for the whole group.

**Bessie Smith Cultural Center**

- Deadlines: Not published. Capture it the first time a guide is booked.
- Access: Fully accessible. Free accessible parking in the lot at Houston and M.L. King Boulevard.

**Tennessee Aquarium**

- Deadlines: Meals and snack packs are ordered ahead and are not refundable inside a week of the visit. Submitting the group request form does not itself hold a reservation — a group arriving on the strength of the form will not be admitted as a group.
- Access: The operator publishes a separate accessibility and inclusion page; nothing group-specific is published.

**High Point Climbing and Fitness**

- Paperwork: Every climber signs a waiver, and anyone under eighteen needs one signed by a parent. Waivers can be completed online in advance.
- Deadlines: Not published. The binding lead time here is the waiver return, not the booking.
- Access: Not published. Ask before selling the line to a group with wheelchair users.

**Cirque de la Symphonie with the Chattanooga Symphony**

- Deadlines: A convenience fee is added to online and telephone orders. Single tickets are not refundable; exchanges may carry a fee. Order the group early enough that an exchange is not the fallback.
- Access: Not published for group bookings; the venue is a historic auditorium.
- Payment: A convenience fee applies to online and telephone orders.

**Point Park**

- Fee: The shape is what matters and the figure belongs in the trip record: a coach of students enters free if the academic fee waiver is asked for a fortnight ahead, and pays at the gate per adult if it is not. This line is easy to print as a free stop and then pay for twice.
- Deadlines: Two weeks ahead for the academic fee waiver. Miss it and the line costs money that was never in the package.
- Access: Park paths and the overlook; the National Park Service publishes accessibility separately.
- Payment: Card only at the gate at present.

**Ross's Landing and the Passage**

- Access: Open public riverfront plaza with steps and ramps down to the water.

**Walnut Street Bridge**

- Access: Level open deck for its full length, with ramps at each end; exposed to weather.
- On the ground: The group crosses on foot and the coaches stay downtown, so a North Shore line on the far side needs a coach plan of its own: either the coaches reposition or the group walks back. This is the kind of detail that only ever gets written in pen.

**Chattanooga Choo Choo**

- Deadlines: Not published. Capture the deposit and final-numbers shape the first time a dinner is booked here.
- Access: A level campus in a converted station; individual venues vary.
